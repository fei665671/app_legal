常见问题

如何开始传文件？

在 iPhone / iPad 上打开本应用，进入「传输」并点击开始接收，记下屏幕上显示的 PIN。在 Mac 客户端填写该 PIN，或通过同一 Wi-Fi 下的浏览器访问手机显示的本地地址。Mac 端可发送文件到手机，或从手机下载到电脑。

Mac 上填哪个 PIN？

请填写手机接收界面上显示的 PIN，不是 Mac 系统设置里的密码，也不是 Mac 应用设置里的 PIN。

数据线极速模式连不上？

请确认 iPhone 与 Mac 已通过 USB 连接，手机保持解锁并开启「开始接收」。若仍无法发现设备，尝试重新插拔数据线、关闭并重新开启接收，或改用同一 Wi-Fi 网络传输。

提示需要「本地网络」权限？

请在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 本地网络」中允许本应用。Mac 首次连接时也可能弹出本地网络授权，请选择允许。拒绝该权限将无法在同一网络下发现设备或传输文件。

隐藏文件夹在 Mac 上看不到？

隐藏文件夹需先在手机上完成保险柜设置并在本机解锁后，Mac 端刷新列表才会显示。这是预期行为，用于保护隐私。

需要注册账号吗？数据会上传云端吗？

不需要注册。文件保存在你的设备本地，传输走局域网或 USB 链路，不经过我们的服务器。详见隐私政策。

传输过程中可以切换 Mac 页面吗？

Mac 应用在发送或下载进行中会提示你留在当前页面，以免传输被中断。完成后可正常切换「发送 / 下载 / 设置」。

Frequently asked questions

How do I start transferring files?

On iPhone or iPad, open the App, go to Transfer, and tap Start Receiving. Note the PIN on screen. On Mac, enter that PIN, or use a browser on the same Wi-Fi to open the local address shown on the phone. The Mac app can send files to the phone or download from the phone to your Mac.

Which PIN should I enter on Mac?

Use the PIN shown on the phone’s receiving screen — not your Mac login password, and not the PIN in Mac Settings inside the App.

USB Turbo mode does not connect

Make sure the iPhone is connected by USB cable, unlocked, and still in Start Receiving mode. Try reconnecting the cable, toggling receiving off and on, or fall back to the same Wi-Fi network.

Local Network permission prompt

On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and allow PhoneDrive. Mac may also ask for local network access on first use — choose Allow. Without it, discovery and transfer on your LAN will not work.

Hidden folders missing on Mac

Hidden folders appear on Mac only after you set up the vault on the phone and unlock it there, then refresh the list on Mac. This is intentional for privacy.

Do I need an account? Is my data uploaded?

No sign-in is required. Files stay on your devices; transfers use your LAN or USB link and do not pass through our servers. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I switch Mac tabs during a transfer?

While sending or downloading, the Mac app asks you to stay on the current page so the transfer is not interrupted. You can switch tabs again after it finishes.
