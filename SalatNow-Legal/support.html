Common topics

  • Prayer times differ from another app → check Calculation Method and madhhab in the app.
  • No reminders → allow Notifications in iOS Settings, then enable reminders in the app.
  • Qibla inaccurate → calibrate the compass; avoid metal cases and magnetic interference.
  • Widgets / Live Activity → add from Home Screen or Lock Screen; enable Live Activity in Notification Settings.
